# Can eye movements tell us what a scene means?

Where people look is a readout of what they find meaningful — so gaze should be predictable
from semantics alone. Ongoing work with Sophie Su uses **CLIP embeddings** together with eye
fixation maps to characterize the semantic content of a film as it unfolds, and to ask how
much of viewers' gaze it explains.

*Su, Upadhyayula & Zacks (in preparation).*
