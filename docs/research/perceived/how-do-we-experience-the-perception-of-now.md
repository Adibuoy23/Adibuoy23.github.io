# How do we experience the perception of *now*?

Not all at once. By breaking the synchrony between foveal and peripheral vision in an RSVP
task, we found that the moment you experience as the present is stitched together from
present information at the center of gaze and **past** information from the periphery. A
moment of visual experience is not a moment in the world.
