# Are key moments just event boundaries?

Partly, but not entirely — and the usual way of comparing them cannot tell. Event
boundaries are typically collected in real time, with a button press; key moments
retrospectively, by picking frames. Construct and measurement method are confounded.

So we crossed them: a fully crossed 2 × 2 of **construct** (key moments vs. event
boundaries) × **procedure** (real-time button press vs. retrospective storyboard
selection).

Two things came out. **The constructs are partially distinct** — equated for task, people
agreed more with each other *within* a construct than *between* them. And **the procedure
matters in its own right**: which psychophysical method you use independently shapes how
responses are organised in time, so the two paradigms are not interchangeable. They may tax
partially nonoverlapping mechanisms, which makes methodological precision essential for
mapping how we perceive and remember events.
